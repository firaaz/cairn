### What and Why

The Claude Code harness drifted from 2.1.114 to 2.1.116 and added ~583 tokens of system-prompt overhead outside cairn''s control, pushing INV-004 turn-1 measurement to 30170-30351 vs. the 30,000 hard budget. Integration sweep #22 (section 1, 2026-04-21) classifies this as environmental drift, not cairn content growth — D1 baseline (27314) and cairn-owned session-start content are unchanged across the window. Trimming cairn content would erode real headroom to mask harness drift and is explicitly wrong.

This slice follows the direct pattern of the prior `housekeeping/inv004-rebaseline` (2026-04-16, commit `d65e50c`) which re-baselined from 22k→30k for the CC 2.1.110 bump. It raises `BUDGET_HARD` to 40,000, updates the three coupled structural tests (architecture paragraph, invariant-check block description, docstring), amends INV-004 in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` to cite the new ceiling plus the CC 2.1.116 measurement, and regenerates the measurement file via the test run. The +36% ceiling convention (22→30 under prior slice) extends to 30→40 here, restoring comfortable headroom above the observed 30351 peak and absorbing the next minor-version bump without immediate re-baseline.
