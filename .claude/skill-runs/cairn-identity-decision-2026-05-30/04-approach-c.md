## 3 — Pre-mortem exposure: C structurally avoids the three high×high/high×med-high scenarios

The pre-mortem's three top-ranked scenarios (`03-premortem.md` S1, S2, S3) are the arc's most severe. **Each is a failure of *canonizing a methodology claim*. C canonizes no methodology claim. So C structurally avoids all three** — not by mitigation, but by not making the bet that triggers them.

- **S1 — "Headline value proves unmeasurable; identity ADR becomes a one-way door defended by prose" (high × high; `03-premortem.md:27`).** S1's mechanism: the ADR canonizes "goal-commitment vs reactivity" as a co-equal value, then 12 months on nobody can measure that the charter cycle reduced reactivity (the failure mode is a counterfactual with no baseline), *and* the ADR has already superseded 3 firm ADRs + retired INV-003 (`03-premortem.md:29`), so the one-way door is paid against an unmeasurable hinge. **C avoids S1 on both legs.** C canonizes *no* methodology headline value — it canonizes the substrate (C5–C8), whose load-bearing-ness arc A already established and which is *demonstrable today* (the validator currently exits 1 on a real coherence violation — `landscape.md:102`; the append-only guard fires session-wide — `02-journey-trace.md:17`). And C retires *no* firm ADR (§2), so there is no expensive one-way door to defend with prose. The cross-cutting observation `03-premortem.md:59` — "even if every mechanism ships perfectly, the methodology defends the wrong axis for its own stated identity" — is a charge that **only bites A**: it is a charge that the *methodology headline* defends the wrong axis. C makes no methodology headline, so the charge has no target.

- **S2 — "Cross-cycle ADR↔code drift forms anyway; per-cycle goal-commitment never touched the corpus-level cliff" (high × high; `03-premortem.md:31`).** S2's mechanism: the charter cycle is a *within-cycle* discipline, but cairn's actual cliff (C1) forms *across the slice corpus* over months (`landscape.md:110`), and the cycle "did NOTHING about this." **C avoids S2 by identity-construction.** C's *named identity* IS the cross-slice coherence contract (C6's append-only ADR substrate + invariant validator) — precisely the corpus-level layer S2 says the methodology cannot touch. C does not promise a within-cycle discipline as the headline; it promises the substrate that operates *at the corpus level*. S2 is the gap between a within-cycle headline and a cross-cycle cliff — C closes that gap by making the cross-cycle substrate the identity in the first place. (Honest limit: C does not *solve* semantic drift either — §7-tier-3 never mechanizes it, F7 confirms it is unmechanizable, and `landscape.md:90` admits it is "unsolved in cairn too." But C does not *claim* to solve it, where A's headline implicitly over-promises by pointing the identity at the wrong axis. C's silence on semantic drift is honest; A's is a mis-aimed headline.)

- **S3 — "`done:`-singularity + same-model verify rubber-stamps; verify-worker certifies build-worker's shared blind spot" (high × med-high; `03-premortem.md:35`).** S3's mechanism: build≠check buys role-PURITY, not error-DECORRELATION; a same-model fresh-context reviewer shares ~60% weights-level blind spots with the builder (Kim et al., `landscape.md:108`); L-016 already FIRED on the downstream consumer; and the charter cycle "removes the 4-phase Auditor *and* the human role-reset half … replaces them with one same-model blind verifier — strictly less decorrelation than the machinery it supersedes" (`03-premortem.md:37`). **C avoids S3 by not making build≠check the one mandatory canonical cleave.** A elevates a single same-model blind-verify step to *the* mandatory invariant (brainstorm-1 §8), canonizing the weakest correlated-error defense (`03-premortem.md:61`). C makes no such claim: it ships the *infrastructure* (the `/decision` C7 protocol with cross-session blind independence; the validator gate) and leaves the *number and shape of verification cleaves* to whichever preset a consumer selects — a consumer needing decorrelation can run the 4-phase preset's 4 roles, or a cross-model preset, without cairn having canonized a single weaker cleave as universal. **C makes no build≠check bet at all** — so S3, which is a charge against canonizing one specific cleave, has no target in C.

`03-premortem.md:63` states it plainly: "Three scenarios (S1, S3, S6) are independently sufficient to force re-supersession within 12 months — the one-way-door cost is paid against a 3-way risk of having to unwind it." **C pays no firm-ADR one-way-door cost (§2) and triggers none of S1/S2/S3.** Of the residual scenarios, S6 (migration straddle) is *lighter* under C (no INV-003 retirement → the consumer's `validate_phase_topology` does not go green-by-vacuity, `03-premortem.md:49`; the 4-phase preset stays valid for unmigrated consumers); S5 (`amendments:` bloat) and S7 (constraint mis-curation) are *preset-level* concerns that bind only the presets that adopt the charter, not cairn's identity. C's pre-mortem surface is dramatically smaller than A's.

### …but does C re-live `identity-and-scope-deferral`? (the honest confrontation)

The operator is left with *two* working methodologies plus BYO — strictly more optionality than today, not less. "No bundled *canonical* preset" ≠ "no methodology." The worst-outcome framing conflates "cairn does not *declare one preset to be its identity*" with "cairn ships no methodology." C does the former, not the latter. The operator who wants a methodology selects one in one line; what they lose is only the *false* claim that the selection is "what cairn is."

The "doc-only is cheaper" escape hatch is closed for C for the *same structural reason* the constraint envelope says it is closed for the charter cycle (`01-constraint-envelope.md:85`): doc-only cannot ship a new primitive with a validator binding.

2. **Arc A provides EVIDENCE A3 never had — and the evidence is what *licenses* C's specific demotion.** A3-2026-05-20 "preserved `phase-lock`" as a hedge, with no basis for deciding *which* legs were durable — it was a reframe in the dark. 3. **The operator's own direction doc already speaks methodology-as-preset.** `2026-05-22-cairn-identity-direction.md:14` (TL;DR): *"Cairn IS infrastructure + intent-file primitive + a methodology preset… The methodology is one preset using this infrastructure, not what cairn IS."* The *only* substantive gap between that sentence and C is the word "a" (one bundled preset) vs C's "presets, plural / BYO." ## 5 — Honest weakness (the single strongest reason a skeptic rejects C)

**C is deferral wearing an infrastructure costume; the operator opened this arc precisely to COMMIT to a methodology, and C dodges the question the arc exists to answer.**

This is the fatal risk and I will not hide it. `context.md` decision #2 is unambiguous: *"Methodology replacement — adopt the **charter cycle**… superseding the 4-phase TDD machinery."* The arc's *posture* is "HARDEN + ATTACK… Charter cycle is the leading proposal" (`context.md:13`). The operator did not open a `/decision` to be told "pick whichever preset you like later." They opened it to *replace the methodology* — to end the methodology question, not to convert it into a menu. C answers a question the operator did not ask (what is cairn's identity-substrate?) and declines the one they did (which methodology?). Three sharp forms of the charge:

1. **The menu re-opens the reactivity the arc is trying to kill — at the methodology layer.** Brainstorm-1 §1 names the target failure as "input-reactive over-steering": a goal that whipsaws instead of accreting. C's "select-or-BYO-per-cycle" arguably *institutionalizes* whipsaw at the level *above* the cycle — every new work-unit re-opens "which methodology this time?" The operator wanted *one committed way of working with inertia*; C hands back a perpetually-re-litigable choice. (Rebuttal C would offer: the demoted presets are documented and stable, and selecting once per project is not per-message whipsaw — but the skeptic is right that C trades a committed methodology for an open one, and that is the opposite of the arc's stated intent.)

2. **"Methodology-agnostic infrastructure" is a thinner identity than the operator signaled.** The direction doc says "infrastructure + intent-file primitive + **a** methodology preset" (`2026-05-22-cairn-identity-direction.md:14`) — it includes *a* preset in the identity. C deletes the "a preset" clause and ships pure infrastructure + primitive. A skeptic reads this as C unilaterally *thinning the operator's own stated identity* past what they asked for — the mirror image of A over-committing. The operator's "complement" starting position (`context.md:7`) presumes a methodology value rides *alongside* the cliff; C has no methodology value to ride alongside anything.

3. **R1 may not be as dead for C as §4 claims.** The skeptic's strongest counter to §4: the intent-file primitive is *not novel to C* — it is brainstorm-1's charter, which *every* approach inherits. A ships the primitive *and* a cycle; C ships the primitive *and demotes two firm ADRs to presets*. Strip the primitive (common to all) and what is *uniquely* C? Two `status:` frontmatter edits demoting `phase-lock`/`phase-pipeline-eval` to "one preset," plus the two provisional supersessions. That is dangerously close to "A3 plus arc A's evidence as justification" — i.e. R1's "rebranding, not redesign" *might* still describe C's *unique* delta once the shared primitive is factored out. §4's rebuttal (arc A *licenses* the demotion; the demotion is re-stratification not re-labeling) is real but not airtight: re-stratifying by documentation status, with the primitive doing the buildable work that all approaches share, is the thinnest *uniquely-C* mechanical change in the arc.

**The honest weight:** C's weakness is *intent-misfit*, not *design-incoherence*. The disconfirming search in 2026-05-20 found A3 "internally consistent" and rejected it on *timing*, not coherence (`identity-and-scope-deferral.md:96`). C inherits that profile: it is internally coherent, constraint-light, pre-mortem-safe, and option-value-maximal — and its fatal risk is that **the operator convened this arc to commit to a methodology, and C's deepest move is to refuse to.** If the lead judges that the arc's purpose is to *end* the methodology question, C loses on purpose-fit regardless of how clean it is on every other axis. If the lead judges that arc A's evidence makes committing *any* methodology premature — that the honest read is "the substrate is the identity; the methodology is the erodable layer we should not yet canonize" — then C is the only approach that says so, and R2 is its endorsement.
