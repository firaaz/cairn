## §5 — Mechanism constraints (invariant-binding-strategy + schema-amendment-threshold)

### What `invariant-binding-strategy` permits/forbids (F6)
- **Validator-TYPE whitelist is closed at 5:** `grep`, `file-exists`, `test-ref`, `git-log-walk`, `structural-parser` (implemented); `ast`, `custom` are reserved/v2-deferred (`invariant-binding-strategy.md:35-41,408-414`). Any mechanism the identity ADR proposes that needs a NEW assertion type must land its own binding ADR — it cannot be smuggled in.
- **Consequence for §7-tier-1 (selection, "ships now"):** "declared `constraints:` respected" must be checkable with one of the 5 types. Feasible: `structural-parser` over the charter file (required `goal:`/`done:`/`constraints:` fields, append-only `amendments:`), or `test-ref` to a charter-schema test. The §7 (Gate) validator coupling reuses the *existing* graph-coherence check (Checks A–D, why-cairn §4) — no new type needed for that.
- **Consequence for §7-tier-2 (smart retrieval) and tier-3 (semantic alignment):** both are *semantic*. invariant-binding-strategy forbids a semantic assertion type at firm status (no `custom`/LLM type exists; D7 rejects even tiktoken to keep the validator offline+self-contained). This is *concordant* with brainstorm-1 §7-tier-3 ("never mechanized") and slice-intent-contract's "no LLM-judge" must-not-violate (P2) — the validator architecturally CANNOT do semantic alignment, which is why discipline-only is the only available answer, not merely the chosen one. **Strengthen the ADR with this: semantic alignment is unmechanizable *given the firm validator-type whitelist*, not just deferred by preference.**
- **`structural-parser` schema lives in the ADR (D4, Consequences "Harder"):** if the charter file gets a structural-parser binding, its schema is ADR-pinned and schema changes are ADR-gated — exactly the firmness brainstorm-1 wants for the charter (append-only like ADRs).

### What `schema-amendment-threshold` permits (F7) — the §3 deferral reuse
- **D2 N≥3 is reused correctly by direction-doc §3** for the smart-retrieval mechanism trigger. Fit check against D2's definitions:
  - **"Independent" = different author-context / different slice-arc** (`schema-amendment-threshold.md:53`). Direction-§3's four misalignment-instance criteria (superseded-ADR ship / active-clause contradiction / omitted-ADR / operator-reported drift) must be counted as *independent* instances — two misalignments in one cycle ≠ N=2. The ADR must say so explicitly or the trigger is gameable (mirrors **L-004**: a counter reused across domains may not measure what its warning text claims — re-audit that "misalignment instance" is the same shape as "schema-drift instance").
  - **"Need" = no canonical alternative home** (`:56`). For mechanism-deferral the analog is "the misalignment could not have been prevented by the existing surfacing discipline" — i.e., a true recall failure, not operator inattention. Criterion #3 (omitted ADR) is the cleanest fit; #4 (self-reported drift) is the weakest (subjective, prose-only-enforced — `schema-amendment-threshold.md` R3 names exactly this: "operator may not file the mark, defeating the counting mechanism").
- **D2 is firm but revisable** (`:97`, R2): "N=3 may be wrong direction for high-importance single instances." A *single* catastrophic misalignment (e.g., a charter ships against a superseded FIRM ADR and corrupts the substrate) might justify mechanism before N=3 — the ADR should carve this out rather than rigidly wait for 3.
- **L-023 cost-asymmetry interaction:** schema-amendment-threshold's "wait for N≥3" and L-023's "cheap probe beats argument" both push the same direction here — don't build the retrieval mechanism speculatively; let cheap empirical signal (actual misalignments) decide. The §3 deferral is well-grounded in *two* firm patterns, not one. This is the deferral's strongest support.
- **L-024 (the lesson form of schema-amendment-threshold)** reinforces: WIDEN-on-N=1 "erodes the firm/provisional distinction." Applied here: shipping a mechanism on the first misalignment would be the mechanism-analog of WIDEN-on-N=1. Deferral is the disciplined default.

### Net mechanism verdict
The charter cycle's three-tier mechanism split (§7) is **fully inside** what the two firm ADRs permit: tier-1 selection is bindable in the existing validator-type whitelist; tier-2/tier-3 semantic work is *architecturally* unmechanizable under that same whitelist (so deferral/never-mechanize is forced, not just chosen); and the N≥3 deferral trigger correctly reuses schema-amendment-threshold D2 — provided the ADR (a) defines "independent misalignment instance" to survive the L-004 counter-reuse trap, and (b) carves out a single-catastrophic-instance fast-path per D2's own R2 caveat.
