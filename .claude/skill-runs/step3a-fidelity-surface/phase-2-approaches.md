## Recommendation (to Phase 3 for stress-test)
**E — commit the right-by-construction core; defer A/B/C** (C prioritized because decoy-catch-rate is the one engagement metric that escapes S6's data-starvation).

Rationale: (1) **E dominates D** — it acts on the *knowable* now (a clause matching no operator-intent line is wrong by construction) instead of waiting for defect-escape data S6 says can't accrue. (2) **E sidesteps the trilemma + metric trap** by making its load-bearing claim a deterministic structural property, not an engagement/recall/decorrelation hypothesis. (3) **E is L-028-complete on the structural axis** and honest about the semantic hole it does NOT close. (4) The supersession is defensible: it ships only the subset whose correctness is independent of the deferred *value* question, keeping the unmeasured-value machinery (compression, challenger-as-load-bearing) deferred — exactly "commit the invariant, defer the machinery."

**Honest residual carried to Phase 3:** E makes erosion *visible*; it does not make the operator *engage*. Whether "bound the blast radius" is the right goal — vs C's "force engagement" or D's "admit you can't and just measure" — is the live question Phase 3 must attack.

**Firmness:** provisional (consistent with `intent-management-loop` + `intent-contract-cost-model` being provisional; the engagement/value questions are explicitly unresolved). → Phase 5 (firm-only) skipped.
