## Findings

### 1. Stale hardcoded ADR list in `test_adr_rename_sweep` — **real defect, new work**

`tests/unit/test_adr_rename_sweep.py` hardcodes an `expected_ids` set that excludes `compression-infrastructure-bootstrap.md`. The ADR landed in `5c4055d` (before compression/infrastructure Phase 1), so this test has been failing since that commit. Sweep #19 was in-slice and evidently did not fail on this; sweep #18's narrative did not note it (ran on a prior tip without the ADR). Two failures share this root cause:
- `TestV1RenameOutcome::test_exact_twelve_adr_files_total` — expects exactly 12 ADR files, sees 13.
- `TestV5IndexShape::test_every_id_column_is_known_flat_slug` — sees `compression-infrastructure-bootstrap` in `docs/adr/index.md` but not in `expected_ids`.

**Class:** `pre-existing` (landed in compression/infrastructure window, before this sweep's slice).
**Fix:** update expected_ids, or convert to a directory-derived set. Small follow-up slice candidate: `housekeeping/adr-rename-test-rebaseline`.

### 2. State-machine tests read real `slice.yaml` — **real defect, known (handoff finding #6)**

**Class:** `pre-existing` (landed Phase 2 of compression/infrastructure).
